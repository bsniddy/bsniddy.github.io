OPERATOR

I'm a Computer Science student at Purdue's John Martinson Honors College, concentrating in Machine Intelligence with minors in Mathematics and Economics.

My work keeps landing in the same place: the seam between software and the physical world. At Iridium that means transceiver firmware and a solar charging subsystem for satellite hardware. At Burbio it meant teaching a pipeline to read a thousand school calendars the way a person does. In the lab it means watching a 3D printer's melt front through a thermal camera and changing the machine's behavior mid-print.

I got to Purdue on the Chick Evans Scholarship — a full tuition and housing award for golf caddies. I now serve as Vice President of New Scholars for that same house, which mostly means I care a lot about people getting a fair shot at the room they're in.

— Brody
